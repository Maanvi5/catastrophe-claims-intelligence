# Product Definition

## Product

Catastrophe Claims Intelligence

## Goal

Make catastrophe claims handling faster and easier by giving claims teams the right context before and after a major weather event.

## Primary user

Catastrophe claims manager / claims team lead.

## The problem

A major weather event creates two connected challenges.

Before the event, the claims team needs to understand potential exposure and prepare resources.

After the event, claims can arrive quickly and in large numbers. The team needs to decide which claims need attention and what level of assessment they require.

Relevant information can come from weather, event data, property information and claims data.

The product should connect these two moments rather than treating them as separate workflows.

## Product flow

### 1. Prepare

A significant weather event is developing.

The claims team can see:

- Expected event severity
- Potentially affected areas
- Insured exposure
- Expected claims volume
- Expected loss severity
- Areas where additional claims capacity may be needed

The goal is to help the team prepare.

### 2. Event

The event occurs.

Expected information can be compared with observed event conditions and emerging impact.

### 3. Triage

Claims begin arriving.

The claims team can see:

- Claim information
- Property information
- Event impact
- Environmental context
- Damage severity
- Available evidence

The goal is to help the team decide which claims need deeper assessment and what handling path is appropriate.

## Job to be done

> When a major weather event is developing or has occurred, help me understand where the impact is likely to be and which claims need my team's attention, so I can prepare and allocate limited claims capacity effectively.

## Product hypothesis

If relevant event, environmental, property and claims information is brought together in one workflow, claims teams can prepare more effectively before an event and make faster, better-informed handling decisions after it.

## MVP

The MVP will have two connected views:

### Prepare

A portfolio-level view of an upcoming weather event showing expected exposure and operational impact.

### Triage

A claim-level view showing incoming claims, relevant event/environmental context and a suggested handling path.

## Handling paths

- Remote review
- Desk assessment
- Field assessment
- Specialist review

The recommendation supports the claims team's judgement. It does not make the final claims decision.

## Out of scope

- Claim approval or rejection
- Final settlement calculation
- Fraud detection
- IBNR modelling
- Building a new weather forecasting model
- Building a full catastrophe model
- Replacing the insurer's claims management system
- Real insurer system integrations

## Design principle

The product should help the claims team move from:

Expected impact → Observed impact → Claims response

without losing the context gathered at each stage.

## Next step

Define the MVP requirements, data model and user stories, then build the prototype.
