# Product Definition

## User

Catastrophe claims manager / claims team lead.

## Problem

After a major weather event, claims volume can increase rapidly. The claims team needs to decide which claims need deeper assessment and where to use limited adjusting capacity.

Relevant information may be spread across claims, property, event and environmental data.

## Job to be done

> When a catastrophe generates a large number of claims, help me quickly understand which claims need attention, what evidence supports that decision, and what should happen next.

## Product hypothesis

If we bring claim, property and environmental information together around each claim, we can help claims teams make faster and more consistent handling decisions.

## MVP question

What is the smallest workflow we can build that proves this hypothesis?

## Out of scope

- Replacing the insurer's claims management system
- Automatically approving or rejecting claims
- Determining final settlement amounts
- Building a new weather forecasting model
- Building a full catastrophe modelling platform
